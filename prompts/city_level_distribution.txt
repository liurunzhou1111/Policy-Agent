A city does not respond as one block. Report each distribution as the five standard box-plot statistics, so that your answer can be drawn directly as a box plot:

- **whisker_low** — the lower whisker: the lowest value that is still *not* an outlier, i.e. the smallest value lying within 1.5 x IQR below q25.
- **q25** — the 25th percentile (the bottom of the box).
- **median** — the 50th percentile (the line inside the box).
- **q75** — the 75th percentile (the top of the box).
- **whisker_high** — the upper whisker: the highest value that is still *not* an outlier, i.e. the largest value lying within 1.5 x IQR above q75.

- **0.0** means visitation is unchanged from normal.
- **-1.0** is the floor: it means visits stopped completely. No value may be below -1.0.
